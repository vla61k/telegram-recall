# Telegram Recall
A concept prototype for smarter chat search, featuring reaction and sticker search, saved conversation fragments, and AI-powered chat insights.

My chats are full of little moments worth remembering: funny messages, ridiculous jokes, unexpected replies, and sometimes more emotional conversations. We react to these messages with emojis, send stickers, laugh about them, and then move on. Over time, even the most memorable moments get buried under thousands of newer messages.
